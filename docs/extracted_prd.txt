— PRD v1.0

Executive summary
JLMP is a branded short-link, QR code and click-attribution layer for Jupsoft products,
launched internally first and opened to paying customers only after it proves value. Its
edge is not shortening links; it is connecting a click to a business result inside the Jupsoft
ecosystem, such as a fee paid, an enquiry raised or an admission confirmed.
The MVP ships multi-tenant from day one, so the commercial launch needs no redesign. It
deliberately defers billing, white labelling and heavy analytics infrastructure until usage
justifies them.
Problem statement
Jupsoft products send thousands of links to parents, students and prospects, but cannot
show whether those links led to action. Today these links are long, unbranded or routed
through third-party shorteners.
Cost and length: long URLs push SMS past the 160-character segment limit, raising
message cost.
Trust: parents increasingly ignore generic short links (bit.ly, tinyurl) because they
resemble phishing.

Compliance: TRAI requires URLs in commercial SMS to be whitelisted on the DLT
platform; third-party shortener domains complicate this.
No attribution: schools cannot see whether a fee reminder led to a payment, or an
admission campaign to an enquiry.
Dependency: third-party services can change pricing, rate-limit, expire links or hold
click data outside Jupsoft's control.
The opportunity is to turn every link Jupsoft products send into a measurable, trusted
touchpoint, and to offer that capability to customers as part of the Jupsoft ecosystem.
Vision, positioning and objectives
JLMP's vision is to make every link an organization sends trusted, trackable and tied to an
outcome. It is positioned as the communication and attribution layer of the Jupsoft
ecosystem, not a standalone Bitly competitor.
Positioning statement: JLMP helps organizations send branded, secure links and QR
codes, and see which messages actually drive payments, enquiries and admissions, by
connecting link activity to the Jupsoft systems they already use.
Why this wins where generic shorteners don't: Bitly, Rebrandly, Short.io and open-
source tools compete on price and features. JLMP competes on attribution inside Jupsoft
ERP, Admission CRM, CRM, LMS and DigifyNext campaigns, which generic tools cannot
see.

Domain strategy and SMS compliance
JLMP uses two dedicated short domains, separate from jupsoft.com, so an abuse flag on
public traffic can never break school fee links or company email.
Domain selection rules
5–7 characters before the TLD, easy to read aloud and type from a printed circular.
Recognizably connected to Jupsoft or its purpose, so recipients trust it.
Prefer .in : short, stable under NIXI, familiar to Indian users. Avoid spam-associated
TLDs (.xyz, .top, .tk) and foreign ccTLDs with unpredictable registry rules.
Registrar lock, auto-renewal, DNSSEC and Cloudflare in front, from day one.
SMS DLT compliance
Each short domain must be whitelisted on the DLT platform before use in SMS, for
Jupsoft and for each sender entity (school) where required.
SMS templates must reference the whitelisted domain; the SMS vendor must confirm
the exact whitelisting process before the MVP pilot.

Conventions: idempotency keys on create endpoints, cursor pagination, per-key rate
limits (starting at 60 requests per minute, raised for internal products), and consistent
error codes. Signed webhooks for link events are a Phase 2 item.
Security, abuse prevention and privacy
The biggest operational risk is a short domain being blacklisted for phishing, so abuse
controls are MVP requirements, not later additions.
Security
HTTPS only with HSTS; TLS terminated at Cloudflare and re-encrypted to origin.
Tenant isolation in application code plus PostgreSQL Row-Level Security.
API keys stored as hashes, scoped, rotatable; admin console with strong passwords
and optional two-factor authentication.
Rate limiting at the edge and per API key; WAF rules on the admin and API hosts.
Audit log for every admin and API change; dependency scanning in CI; annual third-
party penetration test before commercial launch.
Abuse prevention
Destination URLs screened against a malicious-URL service on creation and re-
checked on a schedule.
Block redirects to other shorteners (no chaining) and to known phishing patterns.
Public "report this link" page; Super Admin can disable a link or suspend a tenant in one
action.
Internal and public traffic on separate domains so abuse by external tenants cannot
affect school links.
New commercial tenants start with low limits until verified.
Privacy (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)
Default analytics are aggregate: counts by device, country, referrer and date. Raw IP
addresses are not stored.
